Six steps

From file to fabric.

  1. 01ChoosePick PVC for an all-rounder, mesh for wind and scaffolding, or a roller banner for indoor displays. Not sure? Our sector guide helps.
  2. 02SizeType any width and height for PVC and mesh, from 30cm up to 20m × 5m. Roller banners come in three standard widths, all 2m tall.
  3. 03FinishChoose brass eyelets every 50cm, scaffold tube pockets or plain hemmed edges. Eyelets and hems are free.
  4. 04UploadSend your artwork as PDF, AI, EPS, SVG, TIFF, PNG or JPG, up to 250 MB. The order form checks resolution and shape as you upload.
  5. 05ProofOur team checks every file and emails you a proof within one working day. Nothing is printed, and you pay nothing, until you approve it.
  6. 06DeliveredOnce you approve, your banner is printed within 48 hours, finished, checked again and sent to your door.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I pay when I place my order?

No. We check your artwork first and email you a proof. You only pay once you're happy and approve it.

What if my artwork isn't print-ready?

Upload what you have and tell us in the notes. We'll let you know exactly what needs fixing, and our artwork guide covers the basics.

Can I order a custom size?

Yes. PVC and mesh banners can be any size from 30cm up to 20m wide and 5m tall. Roller banners come in 85, 100 and 120cm widths.

Eyelets or scaffold pockets: which do I need?

Eyelets suit fences, railings and walls, tied on with cable ties or bungees. Scaffold pockets slide over 48mm scaffold tube for a tight, flat finish on scaffolding and frames.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Yes, any time before you approve your proof. Just reply to your proof email or contact us.

Which material is best outdoors?

PVC works for most outdoor spots. In exposed, windy places like scaffolding or high fences, choose mesh so the wind passes through.
